# Privacy Policy / Politique de confidentialité — METAR TAF Trainer

## English

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

### 1. Data controller

This app is developed and published by an independent developer. For any privacy question: **marienfournier11@icloud.com**

### 2. Purpose of the app

METAR TAF Trainer is an educational tool that helps you learn to read aeronautical weather reports (METAR and TAF). It provides no navigation information and must never be used to plan or conduct a flight.

### 3. Data stored on your device

This data stays on your device (the app's local storage) and is never sent to a server of ours: we do not operate any.

- Your progress: correct answers by theme, day streak, achievements, questions to review and their review schedule, date of the last daily quiz.
- Your favorite airports and the history of airports viewed in Live mode.
- Your daily reminder preferences. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, with no notification server.
- The state of your Pro subscription, remembered to avoid showing an unnecessary ad.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

### 4. Weather reports (Live mode)

To display a METAR or TAF, the app queries the public service **aviationweather.gov** (Aviation Weather Center, United States) with the ICAO code of the requested airport. As with any internet request, your IP address is visible to that service. No account or personal identifier is sent. Service policy: [https://aviationweather.gov/](https://aviationweather.gov/)

### 5. Third-party services

The app integrates the following services, which collect data under their own policies.

**Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)**
- Usage events with no personally identifiable data: screens viewed, learning sessions started or completed, question results by theme, airport chosen in Live mode, purchase and consent events, yes/no answers to the review prompt.
- Properties: app language, version, subscription status (free or Pro).
- Anonymous crash and performance diagnostics provided by MetricKit (Apple): type of incident, app and iOS version, with no personal data.
- No name, email, location or content you type is sent.
- Until you have answered the consent request, Firebase runs in restricted mode (Google Consent Mode): no identifier is used.
- Retention: up to 14 months.
- Firebase policy: [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

**Google AdMob (Google LLC)**, free version only
- Displays ads. If you allowed tracking, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize ads. Otherwise ads are not personalized.
- AdMob policy: [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

**Unity Ads (Unity Technologies, Inc.)**, free version only
- Displays ads through AdMob mediation. Depending on your consent and tracking choices, Unity Ads may use your device's advertising identifier to personalize and measure ads.
- Unity policy: [https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)

**Google User Messaging Platform (Google LLC)**
- Collects and stores your advertising consent in accordance with the GDPR.
- Google policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Apple (App Store, StoreKit)**
- Fully handles the Pro subscription (monthly, automatically renewed): payment, renewal and cancellation, from your App Store account settings. We never have access to your payment information.

### 6. Ad tracking (App Tracking Transparency)

Before any ad tracking, the app asks for your permission through Apple's system prompt.

- If you accept: your device's advertising identifier may be used to personalize ads and measure their effectiveness.
- If you decline: no tracking takes place and ads are not personalized.
- You can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

### 7. Advertising consent (GDPR)

In the European Economic Area, a consent form is shown at first launch. You can change your choices at any time in the app's Settings > Privacy options. This row appears when regulations require it.

### 8. International data transfers

Firebase, AdMob and Unity Ads are operated by US companies. These transfers are covered by the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. Google privacy framework: [https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks)

### 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.

- Your progress data is on your device: you can view it on the Progress screen and erase it by uninstalling the app.
- You can withdraw your advertising consent at any time (see sections 6 and 7).
- Analytics data is tied to a random installation identifier, not to your identity: for any request about it, contact us.

To exercise your rights: **marienfournier11@icloud.com**. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL).

### 10. Children

The app is intended for people in aviation training. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

### 11. Changes

Any change to this policy will be signalled by updating the date at the top of this document.

### 12. Contact

**marienfournier11@icloud.com**

## Français

**Dernière mise à jour :** 28 septembre 2026

### 1. Responsable de traitement

Cette application est développée et publiée par un développeur indépendant. Pour toute question relative à la confidentialité : **marienfournier11@icloud.com**

### 2. Finalité de l'application

METAR TAF Trainer est un outil pédagogique qui aide à apprendre à lire les messages météorologiques aéronautiques (METAR et TAF). Il ne fournit pas d'information de navigation et ne doit jamais servir à préparer ou conduire un vol.

### 3. Données stockées sur votre appareil

Ces données restent sur votre appareil (stockage local de l'application) et ne sont jamais envoyées à un serveur qui nous appartienne : nous n'en exploitons aucun.

Ces données sont supprimées lorsque vous désinstallez l'application.

### 4. Messages météo (mode Live)

Pour afficher un METAR ou un TAF, l'application interroge le service public **aviationweather.gov** (Aviation Weather Center, États-Unis) avec le code OACI de l'aéroport demandé. Comme pour toute requête internet, votre adresse IP est visible de ce service. Aucun compte ni identifiant personnel n'est transmis. Politique du service : [https://aviationweather.gov/](https://aviationweather.gov/)

L'application intègre les services suivants, qui collectent des données selon leurs propres politiques.

- Propriétés : langue de l'application, version, statut d'abonnement (gratuit ou Pro).
- Diagnostics de plantage et de performance anonymes, fournis par MetricKit (Apple) : type d'incident, version de l'app et d'iOS, sans donnée personnelle.
- Aucun nom, e-mail, position ni contenu que vous saisissez n'est envoyé.
- Tant que vous n'avez pas répondu à la demande de consentement, Firebase fonctionne en mode restreint (Consent Mode de Google) : aucun identifiant n'est utilisé.
- Conservation : jusqu'à 14 mois.
- Politique Firebase : [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

**Google AdMob (Google LLC)**, version gratuite uniquement
- Affiche des publicités. Si vous avez autorisé le suivi, AdMob peut utiliser l'identifiant publicitaire de votre appareil (IDFA) pour personnaliser les annonces. Sinon, les annonces ne sont pas personnalisées.
- Politique AdMob : [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

**Unity Ads (Unity Technologies, Inc.)**, version gratuite uniquement
- Affiche des publicités via la médiation d'AdMob. Selon vos choix de consentement et de suivi, Unity Ads peut utiliser l'identifiant publicitaire de votre appareil pour personnaliser et mesurer les annonces.

**Google User Messaging Platform (Google LLC)**

Avant tout suivi publicitaire, l'application vous demande votre autorisation par la fenêtre système d'Apple.

Dans l'Espace économique européen, un formulaire de consentement s'affiche au premier lancement. Vous pouvez modifier vos choix à tout moment dans Réglages > Options de confidentialité de l'application. Cette ligne apparaît quand la réglementation l'impose.

Firebase, AdMob et Unity Ads sont exploités par des sociétés américaines. Ces transferts sont encadrés par les clauses contractuelles types approuvées par la Commission européenne. Cadre de confidentialité de Google : [https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks)
